6. CONCLUSION
An SR-05 post-processing prototype has been developed covering punctuation restoration, inverse text normalisation and subtitle generation.
XLM-RoBERTa and ByT5-small were evaluated as separate punctuation approaches. Rule-based normalisation was tested on 5033 records. Reference-guided word-level forced alignment was tested on five audio samples, producing 47 aligned words and five SRT files.
Complete SR-03 integration, larger-scale subtitle evaluation and native-speaker validation remain future work.
